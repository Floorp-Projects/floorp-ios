Floorp for iOS — Extension management UI candidate

This candidate contains one signed, app-bundled extension: Dark Reader. In a
normal profile, open Settings > Extensions and verify the new overview is easy
to understand with Dynamic Type and VoiceOver. Dark Reader must appear as an
icon-rich card with its name, version, description, and clear Available,
Enabled, Disabled, Update Available, or Revoked status as applicable. Loading,
empty, error, retry, and in-progress states must not look tappable or permit a
duplicate action.

Tap the available Dark Reader card. The install review must show the Dark
Reader icon and identity, publisher/source/license information, requested
capabilities, privacy/profile explanations, and the warning that site access
starts off. Cancel once and confirm nothing is installed. Open it again, tap
Install once, and confirm progress is visible and only one copy is installed.
After installation, verify the management screen opens with guidance to choose
Site Access; installation must not silently grant access to every website.

On a non-sensitive approved HTTP(S) page, open Dark Reader from the browser
action UI and grant access to the current site. Confirm the page reloads and
becomes dark. The popup must show the real hostname, not `about:`, and must not
claim an ordinary page is browser-protected. Toggle Off and On while the popup
stays open; both the page and popup status must update. Tap Dark Reader
Settings, change an option, reload, and confirm the authenticated bundled
settings page and saved choice work.

Return to Settings > Extensions, open the installed Dark Reader card, and
exercise each available management action: disable/enable, options, normal
site access, Private Browsing opt-in and private site access, network
protection, website, update history, update (if offered), and uninstall.
Buttons and status must update after each action without a stuck spinner,
duplicate sheet, crash, or stale card. Disabling must stop the popup and page
transformation. Re-enabling must restore them without changing generation.
After uninstall and reinstall, site access must again start off and removed
extension-owned settings must not return.

Repeat the install, management, site-access, popup, Settings, and uninstall
paths in Private Browsing. Private use is opt-in and profile-local. Normal
grants, storage, alarms, and runtime state must not leak into private browsing,
or back from private to normal browsing.

Release-integrity checks: Settings > Extensions must show exactly one catalog
package. There must be no Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, arbitrary URL,
ZIP, XPI, CRX, share-sheet, or local-file installation path, and Dark Reader
must not fetch remote code, configuration, updates, or filter lists. Stop and
report a mismatch, extra item, permission bypass, unexpected network activity,
cross-profile leak, crash, hang, or version/build mismatch.

Repeat the core checks in English and Japanese, with larger text and VoiceOver.
Use only approved non-sensitive pages and no personal credentials or
production data.
